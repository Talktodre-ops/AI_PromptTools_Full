# PROMPTHERO: Strategic Roadmap to Product Excellence

> **Mission**: Transform PROMPTHERO from a prompt engineering tool into the leading AI content optimization platform

## 📊 Market Opportunity

- **Market Size**: Prompt engineering market projected to grow from $380M (2024) to $6.5B (2034) at 32.9% CAGR
- **Video AI Gap**: 85% of users struggle with effective prompting for video AI tools (Runway, Pika, Sora)
- **Enterprise Demand**: 72% of companies want to outsource prompt engineering (YC research)
- **Timing**: Perfect moment as video AI becomes mainstream
